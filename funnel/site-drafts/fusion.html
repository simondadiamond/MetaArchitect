Fair questions.

Why can I not just use ChatGPT myself?

You can, and for some things you should. The gap is not the writing. A chat window answers when you open it. It does not run on Tuesday at six in the morning, it does not read the inbox, it does not stop and ask you when it is unsure, and it never tells you whether it saved you anything.

That plumbing is most of the work. Deciding which job is worth the plumbing is the rest of it, and that decision is what the mapping session is for.

What if it does not work?

Two answers, both written into the offer. If the mapping session does not leave you with at least one workflow worth building, you do not pay for it. If the sprint is not doing the job at the end of the fourteen day live run, I keep working at no charge until it is, or the balance is waived.

The deposit covers discovery and is not refundable, and once you accept the work there are no refunds. Saying that plainly now is part of the offer.

How long does it take?

The mapping session is one meeting and a written plan. A sprint is usually two to three weeks of building, then fourteen days running live before the balance is due.

Your share of that is the first session, a handful of questions while I build, and the handoff conversation. If a sprint starts consuming your week, I have designed it wrong.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You need to know the job well enough to tell me when an answer is wrong. That judgment is the only expertise the build depends on, and it is exactly why there is an approval step instead of a black box.

What happens to the C$350 if I go ahead?

It comes off the sprint in full, as long as the sprint is booked within thirty days. If you decide not to go ahead, you keep the written plan and owe nothing else. You are welcome to hand that plan to somebody else and have them build it.

What about my client data?

Ask me in the session, about the specific tools we would actually use, and the answer will be concrete: what leaves your systems, what does not, and what the safer option costs instead. Anyone who answers that question in a FAQ is guessing about your business.
