# Partner roster outreach — CEGEPs, chambers, mutuelles

The play (brief §5): don't build an acquisition machine, rent one. These organisations
already hold SMB rosters, accreditation, and a sales function, and they already receive
the 1% training budget. The ask is **to join their roster of external formateurs**, not
to sell them anything. Rate: $1,200–3,000/day subcontracted, acquisition cost zero.

Targets, in order: CEGEP Limoilou, CEGEP Sainte-Foy, Cégep de Lévis (services aux
entreprises / formation continue). Then Université Laval formation continue, CCI de
Québec, the Lévis chamber, mutuelles de formation, ADRIQ.

**French only.** These are Quebec public institutions.
**Simon reviews the French before sending** — written by an agent, not a native speaker.

## Why it's shaped this way

- **One ask.** "What's your process" is answerable in one line by someone who has the
  answer, and forwardable by someone who doesn't.
- **The outline is withheld.** Offering it rather than attaching it gives them a reason
  to reply and keeps the first email short.
- **No pitch, no price.** Rates come up after they've decided you're worth adding.
- **Local first.** "Je suis à Lévis" does more work in Quebec than any credential.

## The two-hour workshop outline (send on reply)

Built so the CEGEP can sell it, which means it has to solve *their* problem: a catalogue
with no credible AI offering.

1. **Where AI actually saves time in an SME — and where it doesn't.** Named examples from
   real operations. The honesty is the hook.
2. **The three things that break in production.** Silent wrong answers, no logging, no
   human gate before consequential actions. This is STATE translated for owners; the
   framework is never named.
3. **Live build.** One workflow from the room mapped and automated in front of them.
   This is the part no other formateur on their roster can deliver.
4. **Buy vs build.** What existing software already solves, what's worth custom work.
5. **What it costs and how Québec funds it.** MFOR at 50–75% for new-technology training,
   the 1% obligation, Tr@ns Num. The CEGEP loves this section — it helps them close.

Deliverable for attendees: a one-page worksheet scoring their own recurring work on
frequency, time, and cost of error.

## After the first delivery

Credibility transfers. Ask for: a named contact at two client firms who attended, and
permission to reference the institution by name. That's the bridge from rented
distribution to direct Phase 1 clients.
