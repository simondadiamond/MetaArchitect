**The approved system, concretely:**

- **Palette** — warm drafting-paper background (`#F3EEE1` paper / `#EAE3D0` deep panel), warm ink text (`#1C1712` ink / `#5B5346` soft / `#948C7A` faint), brass/bronze for labels and detail accents (`#8C6A2F`), brand orange `#E04500` kept but spent in exactly two places only: button hover/press, and one full-bleed dark contrast panel at the very end of the page. This is a deliberate break from "always dark mode" — **for operator surfaces only**. Practitioner lane (`/score`, blog pillars Defensive Architecture / Regulated AI & Law 25, `LegacyHomePractitioner.tsx`) keeps the original dark/raw/blocky system unchanged; that system is arguably correct for a burned-engineer audience, per the same research.
- **Typography** — Merriweather (serif, kept deliberately — Simon likes it, and a confident serif is currently a differentiator against the sea of grotesk AI/SaaS sites) for display/headlines with italic used for emphasis words; Inter for body. No monospace anywhere on operator surfaces — mono reads as a technical/practitioner-audience tell.
- **No pill buttons, no bordered card grids, no glow orbs, no colored accent bars on cards.** These were all tried and explicitly rejected this session. Rectangular buttons (solid ink fill, orange on hover, scale-down on click for a tactile "press" feel) instead.
- **Signature devices:**
  - *"Reset vs. remembers"* — two physical note cards (background-differentiated, real box-shadow, each rotated a degree or two off-axis, a small torn-tape accent pinning each down) contrasting a chat that resets ("Hi, I run a coaching business, and—" repeated/greyed) against a workspace that remembers (Voice / Rates / Clients, persisted). This replaced an earlier idea — an animated architectural floor-plan diagram of "the business" — that was deliberately dropped mid-session: it was distinctive but asked cold, non-technical traffic to do real decode work before getting the payoff, which is a genuine clarity risk this ICP can't afford. **Lesson for future design work on this brand: a clever ownable metaphor is worth less than instant comprehension for a first-time, non-technical visitor.** If a future revision wants to reintroduce a more elaborate metaphor, pair it with much more explicit inline labeling, or A/B it — don't ship it on instinct alone.
  - *Rate card as a physical sheet* — background lifted off the page with a real drop shadow, tilted slightly (~0.4°) off-axis, a small pin-dot detail top-left, rows separated by numbered leader-dot lines (`01  Working Session ······ $125/hr`) instead of ruled table borders. This replaced a literal bordered 4-box SaaS pricing grid, which read as "a table with borders turned on."
  - *Founding-rate seal* — a circular ink-stamp graphic ("Founding rate · 3 of 3"), not a badge or colored border, marking the $6,500 flagship tier.
  - *Personal signature* — a quiet line near the final CTA attributing the work to Simon by name. This session's independent trust-criteria research (`docs/research/operator-trust-criteria-independent-2026-08-09.md`) found this buyer responds to a person more than a company; use that line, or something equivalent, rather than dropping it for being "extra."
- **A real bug this session found and fixed, worth hunting for elsewhere**: the final CTA originally said "leave with a working setup" attached to a two-hour timeframe, which contradicts the $6,500/~30-day flagship described one section earlier on the same page. Fixed to honest multi-entry-point framing ("One call. No pitch — just a plan for where you actually are — session, audit, or the full setup"). Audit other pages for similar internal contradictions between marketing copy and the real offer ladder in `product-marketing.md`.
- **The practitioner door must stay.** A quiet strip (Score my stack · Blog · Work with me), deliberately less designed than the rest of the page, per `brand-summary.md`'s audience-routing rule that practitioner always keeps a compact door, never gets fully removed. It was accidentally dropped once this session and had to be restored — don't drop it again on any page.

**Design-critique method used this session, reusable for the final pass:** ran the open-source `taste-skill` redesign-audit checklist (github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill — not installed as a Claude skill, fetched via WebFetch) against the build: checks for generic three-equal-card layouts, oversaturated/purple-blue-gradient "AI design fingerprint," bordered/table-like components, cliché copy words ("Elevate," "Seamless," "Unleash"), and missing interactive/focus states. Worth re-running that checklist against the final production pages, not just the mockup.
